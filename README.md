> I asked Dad why he brings a ladder to the bar. He said the drinks are on the house — he’s just trying to reach them.

One dad joke of at most 200 characters for Dad Joke Showdown on make.fun. The program writes it deterministically with no network access to `entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
